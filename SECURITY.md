# Security policy

Report security problems with fairfieldct.ai, its APIs, its Discord bot, or its
infrastructure by email to hello@inbox.fairfieldct.ai. Please don't open a public
issue for a vulnerability.

Include what you found, how to reproduce it, and what an attacker could do with it.
We're volunteers, but we'll acknowledge reports as soon as we can and let you know
when they're fixed.
